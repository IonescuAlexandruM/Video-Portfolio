MULTICAM VIDEOS | VIDEOGRAPHY | LONG-FORM VIDEOS

I’m a videographer documenting the underground — raves, abandoned spaces, raw energy, and the people who make these nights unforgettable.

I’m drawn to places and moments that feel outside the ordinary. My goal is to capture the atmosphere, the chaos, the music, and the stories behind it all — not just as a video, but as a memory of a night that actually happened.

Now I’m looking to take it a step further: documenting and eventually hosting my own events, bringing together music, visuals, and unique spaces to create something of my own.

No polished nightlife bullshit. Just raw energy, good people, loud music, and moments worth remembering.
